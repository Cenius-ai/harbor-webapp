Harbor is a quiet place for thoughtful writing about software engineering, systems design, and the craft of building for the modern web. We publish essays, tutorials, and reflections from practitioners who care deeply about their work.

## What we write about

Our authors explore the full stack — from TypeScript type systems to database architecture, from CSS layout engines to team process and engineering culture. The common thread is depth: every piece aims to go beyond the surface and examine the _why_, not just the _how_.

We believe the best technical writing is timeless. A well-argued essay about API design or testing philosophy remains relevant long after the framework of the month has been replaced. We write for the career, not the release cycle.

## Who writes here

Harbor is written by a small team of experienced engineers who have built systems at scale and want to share what they have learned. Our authors have worked at startups and enterprises, on frontend and backend, in code and in leadership. We write under our own names because we stand behind what we publish.

## About this site

Harbor is built with [Nuxt 3](https://nuxt.com), styled with [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com), and powered by [Nuxt Content](https://content.nuxt.com) — a modern static-site stack that is fast, accessible, and a pleasure to work with. All content is written in Markdown and rendered at build time. The typeface is [Literata](https://www.type-together.com/literata-font), a serif family designed for immersive reading on screens.

The source code for this site is available on GitHub at [github.com/cenius/harbor](https://github.com/cenius/harbor).
